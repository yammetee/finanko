Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 August 2026
evenkvit processes personal financial information across three independent areas: expenses, capital, and debts. This notice explains what is processed, why it is processed, which providers receive data, and how an account can be deleted.

1. Controller and contact

evenkvit is operated as the evenkvit project (“evenkvit”, “we”, “us”). For privacy requests, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, contact finankosupport@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers, session information, account metadata, and the recorded versions and timestamps of accepted legal documents.
  • Expense data: item amounts, currencies, categories, dates, descriptions, input sources, and deletion state.
  • Capital data: user-defined groups, assets, cash and deposit positions, symbols and provider identifiers, events, quantities, prices, fees, taxes, income, interest rules, quotes, and derived valuation snapshots.
  • Debt data: user-defined groups, lender and loan descriptions, balances, rates, required payments, dates, remaining terms, payment events, repayment strategies, reconciliations, and derived projections.
  • Receipt and AI inputs: the receipt image or expense text you choose to analyze, plus the category and currency context needed to prepare an editable result.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, request metadata, security logs, and error information generated by hosting and infrastructure providers.

3. Independent financial areas

Expenses, capital, and debts are deliberately independent. An action in one area does not create, edit, or delete a record in another area. This separation is part of the service design and does not indicate that real-world transactions have been reconciled across all areas.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process account and financial data to provide authentication, synchronization, editing, calculations, history, currency conversion, market valuation, receipt and text analysis, and debt projections requested by you. The principal legal basis is performance of the Terms of Use and steps requested before entering that contract. Security, abuse prevention, reliability, quota enforcement, and limited operational logging rely on legitimate interests. Where applicable law requires consent for an optional feature, consent will be requested separately.

5. AI processing

AI-assisted text and receipt parsing runs only when you request it. The selected text or prepared receipt image, available categories, and currency context are sent through a server-side Vercel function to OpenAI. A receipt may be processed in more than one AI step to read the document and assemble editable expense positions. AI output may be incomplete or incorrect and must be reviewed before saving. Receipt images are not stored in the evenkvit relational database.

6. Market and exchange-rate data

For market-backed capital items, evenkvit may send a symbol or provider asset identifier to Bybit, CoinGecko, Nasdaq, or Yahoo Finance to search for instruments and retrieve current or historical quotes. These requests do not include your stored quantities, balances, events, or debt data. Your browser requests current currency rates from the configured ExchangeRate-API endpoint, which may receive normal network metadata such as your IP address.

7. Service providers and disclosures

  • Supabase: authentication and relational database hosting.
  • Vercel: web hosting and server-side API execution.
  • OpenAI: text and receipt analysis when invoked.
  • ExchangeRate-API: current currency-rate retrieval.
  • Bybit, CoinGecko, Nasdaq, and Yahoo Finance: market instrument search and quote retrieval.

We do not sell personal data, run behavioural advertising, or disclose your stored financial records to data brokers. Data may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

8. International transfers

Providers may process data outside your country of residence. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognised safeguards made available by the relevant provider, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

9. Storage and local device data

Financial records are stored in Supabase and scoped to the authenticated owner. Supabase Auth uses browser local storage or equivalent necessary client storage to maintain the signed-in session. evenkvit does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, appropriate controls will be added before activation where required.

10. Retention and account deletion

Account and financial data are retained while the account remains active. Deleting an individual expense hides it from active expense views but may retain its deleted record until the account is deleted. You can permanently delete the account from the authenticated application. This removes the Supabase Auth user and, through database cascades, the account’s categories, expenses, capital records, quotes, snapshots, debt records, payments, roles, and AI quota records. The operation cannot be undone.

Provider-controlled backups, security logs, and request logs may remain for limited retention periods before their normal deletion cycles complete. Information may also be retained where strictly required by law or for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

11. Security

evenkvit uses authenticated access, owner-scoped row-level security, private database tables for capital and debt data, HTTPS transport, server-side AI and administrative credentials, request validation, and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password and protect access to your email and device.

12. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a portable copy of your personal data, and may complain to your local data-protection authority. We may verify identity before fulfilling a request. Contact the address above.

13. Children

evenkvit is not directed to children under 16 and should not be used by anyone unable to enter the Terms under applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal data.
